# Assessment sources and interpretation

Reviewed on 19 October 2026. Rubric ID: `aqa-8700-writing-reviewed-2026-10-19-v1`.

The marking payload contains an original, concise paraphrase of the writing bands. It is not a copy of a complete AQA mark scheme or a claim of AQA endorsement. All lesson prose, practice examples and task prompts are original.

## Two assessment modes

- Baseline, practice and transfer: task-specific skill feedback; both numeric AO marks must be null.
- Sustained AQA-style response: AO5 integer 0–24, AO6 integer 0–16. The server calculates the total. One task cannot establish GCSE grade 1–9.

AO5 and AO6 are assessed separately by overall fit to the descriptions. The model must not manufacture marks by counting devices, paragraphs, adjectives or errors. The reviewed 2026 Paper 1 off-focus AO5 cap is applied server-side when the model reports the focus is not addressed.

Evidence quotations are checked literally against the current answer before a result is saved. Missing or malformed skills, impossible marks, invented quotations, incomplete JSON and refusals cause a safe failure. These checks prevent particular technical failures; they do not prove that a literary judgement or mark is correct.

## Calibration before depending on marks

Use a small selection of teacher-marked or official publicly available exemplars with the matching task and rubric. Compare AO5 and AO6 separately; include lower, middle and stronger writing, a short response, an off-task response and an unusual but effective style. Record disagreements, not just the total. Calibrate again after a rubric or model change.

No teacher calibration or live OpenAI marking was performed in this build environment. Automated checks use clearly labelled synthetic feedback and mocked HTTP responses, never Greta's or Mathis's writing.

## Independent progress

Revisions always reference their preceding submitted version. A revision never counts as a second independent task. The overview considers up to three recent different independent tasks; two recent secure assessments are required to label a skill secure across tasks. Unobserved skills are left ungraded.

Suggested next lessons are selected from the trusted course, using repeated difficulty across independent pieces where available. An AI response cannot invent a route or silently change the curriculum.
