No duplicate quiz system was added. Chapter 1 is registered in the existing organiser registry, so the existing Science revision route automatically draws from Chapters 1 and 2 according to each student's taught sections.

- Organisers remain sorted by `chapter`, placing Health and Lifestyle above Ecosystem Processes.
- Chapter 1 contributes all nine question modes: MCQ, Short, Long, Matching, Fill in the Blanks, Label the Diagram, Ordering, Classification and Mixed.
- Question IDs are globally distinct (`health-q01`–`health-q75` versus `eco-q...`).
- Existing taught-section filtering, randomisation, result totals, by-chapter results and by-question-type results are unchanged.
- Existing Greta, Mathis, test-account and guest storage keys are unchanged.
- Standard Short and Long questions still use the existing trusted server-side AI route; fixed-answer interactions remain deterministic.
