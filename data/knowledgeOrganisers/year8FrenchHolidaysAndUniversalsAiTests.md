# French AI Marking Test Cases

1. Accept a natural equivalent English translation when meaning is unchanged.
2. Reject an answer with the opposite meaning or missing negation.
3. Reject a wrong subject, tense or auxiliary when the marking point requires the exact form.
4. Require accents, cedilla, ligature or apostrophe only when the trusted rubric makes exact spelling part of the mark.
5. Accept `on` as “we” where the KO uses it, while retaining third-person singular verb agreement.
6. Do not require vocabulary outside the taught KO.
7. For long answers, return strengths, corrections, improvements and a source-bounded model response.
