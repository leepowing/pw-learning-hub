# Privacy and Audio Data Checklist

- Recording starts only after the student presses Start recording.
- The browser requests microphone permission at that point.
- Audio is sent directly to Azure Speech for immediate assessment.
- PW Learning Hub does not write raw audio to logs, analytics, a database or object storage.
- Results are formative and do not change quiz marks, taught status or rankings.
- A denied microphone safely leaves Listen/self-practice available.
- A parent or guardian should review the notice before children first use recording.
