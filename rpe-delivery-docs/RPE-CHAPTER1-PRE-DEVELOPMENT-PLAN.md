## 3. Faithful Chapter 1 content inventory

### A. Covenants — 15 atomic items

1. A covenant is a sacred agreement between God and humans.
2. The first covenant is between God and Abraham and is called the Abrahamic Covenant.
3. God promises Abraham a very large family / many descendants.
4. Abraham's descendants are compared with the stars in the sky.
5. God promises Abraham's descendants a promised land of their own.
6. The covenant between Moses and God renews God's commitment to the Israelites.
7. The Israelites are described as Abraham's descendants.
8. The Israelites agree to follow the rules God sets for them.
9. The rules are presented as protecting them.
10. The Israelites are to spread God's power through the world.
11. They become a “nation of priests”.
12. A key theme in Judaism, Christianity and Islam is that humans may turn away from God's conditions.
13. God keeps his promises even when humans fail.
14. In Christianity, Jesus is understood as renewing and remaking the old covenants.
15. In Islam, Muhammad is presented as the final prophet who makes God's expectations of humans clear.

### B. The Exodus — 23 atomic items

1. Exodus is the name of the second book of the Torah.
2. The Torah is identified as the Hebrew Bible in the KO wording.
3. “Exodus” means “exit”.
4. Exodus describes Moses leading the enslaved Israelites out of Egypt.
5. The movement out of Egypt is a movement into freedom.
6. The Egyptian ruler orders Jewish boys to be killed.
7. The order follows a warning that a Jewish man would threaten his rule.
8. Moses' mother, Yocheved, places him in a basket in the river.
9. Yocheved trusts that God will protect Moses.
10. Moses is discovered by Batya, Pharaoh's daughter.
11. Moses is raised within Batya's household, with his mother involved in his care.
12. Moses kills an Egyptian.
13. Moses is exiled / flees into the desert.
14. Moses encounters the Burning Bush.
15. God commands Moses to liberate the Israelites from slavery.
16. Pharaoh refuses to let the Israelites go.
17. God sends ten plagues to punish the Egyptians.
18. The final plague is the death of the firstborn.
19. The Israelites mark their doors with lamb's blood.
20. Their homes are “passed over”, giving the festival Passover its name.
21. Moses leads his people into the desert.
22. Moses parts the Red Sea.
23. The Israelites cross towards / into Sinai.

### C. Religious Experiences — 7 atomic items

1. Ineffable: a religious experience is hard or impossible to describe in words.
2. Noetic: a religious experience involves gaining knowledge.
3. Passive: a religious experience happens to a person rather than being controlled by them.
4. Transient: a religious experience does not last long, although its effects may continue.
5. Religious explanation: the experience is real and may be evidence for something that cannot otherwise be explained.
6. Physical explanation: the experience may be caused by something happening in the brain.
7. Psychological explanation: the experience may be explained through emotions or psychological processes.

The implementation must not silently correct this. Confirm whether the student-facing term should preserve `Defector` exactly or use `Defeater` with a source note.
2. The last Exodus sentence is read as the Israelites crossing towards / into **Sinai** after the Red Sea. This wording should be checked against the original image before final transcription.
3. The source image bytes are not available in the current workspace, so an exact character-by-character transcription and final page-accounting check cannot yet be rerun locally.
4. The current workspace project snapshot is older than the user's deployed project: it does not contain the latest Geography and Design Technology shared files. Shared-file changes therefore cannot safely be applied to this snapshot. The latest versions must be supplied or the package must contain merge-only instructions.
